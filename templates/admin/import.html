What happens: nozzles, tanks, rates, transporters with full history, lube products with current stock, staff, attendance, salary, tank dips, tanker receipts, photos and the old activity log are copied. Every day before today becomes read-only history.

Balances: each transporter’s balance is rebuilt from their old entries and compared with what the old app showed. Any difference is added as one clearly labelled adjustment, and you get a list to check.

How long: usually under a minute; a few minutes for several years of data. A progress bar shows each step.

Do this first: download a backup of the old app (old Settings → Full system export).
